Sprite Grid Builder

Drop downloaded illustrations into individual cells, or tap a cell to choose an image. Move and resize them without distortion, then export a mathematically exact WebP sprite sheet. Images stay on your device.

How to use: Drop images onto cells, or tap an empty cell to upload. Tap a filled cell to select it; drag its picture to reposition. The blue guides are for editing only—they will not be exported. Every image is clipped inside its own cell and the chosen margin.
